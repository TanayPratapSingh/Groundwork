EM and token F1 are reported for comparability with published SQuAD results, but they understate this system by construction: SQuAD's gold answers are minimal spans and this pipeline returns cited sentences, so a fully correct answer loses most of its F1 to tokens that were never in the span. Containment is the metric that tracks what the product needs, and the two are reported together because containment alone can be gamed by answering at length.
